# Content Guidelines

## Voice

Professional, clear, restrained and informative Turkish.

## Never invent

- baro
- sicil
- education
- experience
- awards
- clients
- testimonials
- case results
- expertise
- address
- phone

Use `CONTENT_REQUIRED`.

## Legal content

Avoid guaranteed outcomes.

Distinguish general information from personalized legal advice.

Cite current legislation/jurisprudence only after verification.

AI-generated legal content must be reviewed before publication.

## SEO

Write for search intent and user value, not keyword density.

Avoid city-name substitution at scale.

## CTA

Use restrained CTAs:
- İletişime Geç
- Telefonla Ara
- WhatsApp'tan Ulaş

Do not use deceptive urgency.
