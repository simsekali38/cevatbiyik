# SEO Skill

## Purpose
Implement technically correct and user-first SEO.

## Rules
- crawlability
- indexability
- metadata
- canonical
- sitemap
- structured data
- internal links
- local relevance
- Core Web Vitals

## Do Not
- keyword stuffing
- doorway pages
- fake schema
- fake reviews
- thin city pages
- spam backlinks

## Definition of Done
SEO checklist passes and no critical indexing conflicts remain.
